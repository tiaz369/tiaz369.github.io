三个版本The Three Versions

1888年，梵高在阿尔勒的黄房子里画下自己的卧室，当时他在等高更来同住。后来他住院期间，这幅画受潮损坏。1889年，他在圣雷米重画了两次：一幅同样大小，一幅较小，送给母亲和妹妹。

In 1888, Van Gogh painted his bedroom in the Yellow House in Arles, while waiting for Gauguin. The painting was later damaged by damp while he was in hospital. In 1889, he painted it twice more: one full-size, and a smaller one for his mother and sister.
