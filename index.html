AI & Robotics

What I'm paying attention to.

I'm not an engineer. But I watch closely — following the people building things, reading what I can, trying to understand what's actually changing rather than what's being announced.

Directions
Embodied AI
Robots that operate in the real world. The gap between language and physical grounding is where the hard problems live — and where I think the most interesting things will happen.
AI agent identity
As agents become more capable, how they represent themselves — and how we verify that — becomes urgent. This is connected to what I'm building with Nexart.
Foundation models for robotics
A single model controlling many different robots — generalizing across hardware the way language models generalize across tasks. Following Physical Intelligence, Figure, NVIDIA.

03

Nexart

A project I started. Still in motion.

Nexart started with a simple frustration: the infrastructure connecting artists to collectors — especially women artists in places where opportunity is scarce — was broken or absent. The original idea was to use NFTs as a bridge: provable ownership, global access.

The NFT market moved in ways that made that harder. But while watching the space closely, I noticed something else. As AI agents become more capable, the question of identity becomes critical — how does an agent prove it is who it says it is? How do you give an AI a verifiable, unique identity that persists?

NFTs are actually well-suited to this. So Nexart evolved. I drafted a patent around AI agent identity and filed it in the UK. It's pending. The original mission is still there — the infrastructure question is just unsolved.

Film

Films I return to.

The films I keep coming back to share something: they make you believe in a reality with different rules — not by explaining it, but by committing to it completely.

Interstellar
Nolan · 2014
Love as a force that crosses dimensions — not metaphor, but physics. The tesseract treats emotion and space-time as the same kind of thing. I find that genuinely moving.
Sci-fi
Inception
Nolan · 2010
Consciousness constructs reality. A dream is real if the rules are consistent enough. I think about this more now — an AI's world is also a constructed one.
Thriller
The Lord of the Rings
Jackson · 2001–03
Purity of heart against overwhelming power. Everything in Middle-earth has weight and history — the world feels real because it was built with that kind of care.
Epic
Spirited Away
Miyazaki · 2001
A child enters a world with completely different rules and survives by staying true to herself. The best films don't explain their logic — they make you believe it within minutes.
Animation
Rick and Morty
Harmon & Roiland · 2013–
Rewatched more times than I can count. A genius who has seen every possible universe and still chooses to come home — I can't fully explain why that gets me, but it does.
