About

My name is Linfang Zhong — most people call me Tia. I've spent the past few years helping people navigate some of the biggest decisions of their lives: where to study, where to live, what comes next. That work taught me to read people, tell their stories, and understand systems.

Now I'm applying that same curiosity to a different kind of frontier. I'm genuinely fascinated by AI and robotics — not as buzzwords, but as a fundamental shift in what machines can do and what that means for how we live. I'm at the beginning of that learning journey, and I'm not hiding it.

Outside of work, I watch a lot of films, photograph art in museums, and pay attention to things that most people walk past. I co-founded Nexart, a small startup at the intersection of art and technology.

AI & Robotics

What I'm paying attention to. Updated as I learn.

I'm not a researcher or engineer — yet. But I'm watching closely and trying to understand what's actually happening, not just what's being hyped.

Directions I find exciting
Embodied AI
Robots that can operate in the real world, not just digital environments. The gap between language and physical grounding is where the interesting problems live.
Foundation models for robotics
Following what Jim Fan's group at NVIDIA is building — the idea that a single model could learn to control many different robots feels like a genuine inflection point.
AI + creative industries
How these tools change who gets to make things, and what "authorship" means. Connected to my work with Nexart.
People I follow
Jim Fan
Senior research scientist at NVIDIA. Working on foundation models that can control robots across many environments.
Andrej Karpathy
Ex-Tesla, ex-OpenAI. Makes AI concepts genuinely understandable. His neural network series is on my list.
